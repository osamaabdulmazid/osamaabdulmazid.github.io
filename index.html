THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

More than administration.

Who is Osama Abdul Mazid?

Osama Abdul Mazid is an Administration & Student Affairs professional based in Chattogram, Bangladesh. His work sits at the practical intersection of people, processes, and systems — from admissions and student support to records, documentation, customer service, and campus operations.

He started his working life in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, later built his higher-education journey with the University of the People, and has continued to grow through hands-on roles in education, outreach, and administration. Along the way, he served as a UoPeople Ambassador, supporting prospective students and helping others understand an online path to higher education.

In 2026, he completed a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from the University of the People. He brings that academic foundation into practical work: communicate clearly, keep records accurate, solve the problem in front of you, and make the next step easier for the person depending on you.
